Benefits checklist

Sign up now

ORR says sign up immediately: cash and medical help are limited to a period from your ORR eligibility date [source].

Checklist

  • Bring proof: I-94 showing admission under section 207, or I-94 code Visa 93, or EAD code A03 [source].
  • Mainstream benefits: you may be eligible for TANF, SSI, Medicaid or SNAP [source].
  • Refugee Cash Assistance (RCA): if not eligible for TANF or SSI. Up to 12 months for an ORR eligibility date on or after 1 January 2026 [source, source].
  • Matching Grant: an alternative to TANF or RCA with limited slots; 240-day service period [source].
  • Refugee Medical Assistance (RMA): see Health cover.
  • Domestic medical screening soon after arrival [source].
  • Refugee Support Services: jobs, English, childcare, transport, interpreting, case management, up to 5 years [source].

Where to apply

Your state benefits office or nearest resettlement agency. For local providers, contact your State Refugee Coordinator [source, source].

Conflict between sources

The ACF benefits fact sheet (page dated 9 October 2026) still says four months of RCA and RMA for an eligibility date on or after 5 May 2025 [source]. ORR's Dear Colleague Letter 26-05 (revised 1 October 2026) and the Federal Register correction say up to 12 months for an eligibility date on or after 1 January 2026 [source, source]. The newer policy letter appears to govern; confirm with your provider.
