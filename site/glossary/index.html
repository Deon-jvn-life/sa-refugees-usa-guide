Words and abbreviations explained

Short meanings for words used on this site. If a short line and the official page do not match, follow the official page. The first time a word appears on another page, it links here.

A3 and A03

The refugee code USCIS prints on an Employment Authorization Document. You may also see it written A3 or A03. SSA accepts an EAD in this category as evidence of status. [source, source, source]

ACF

The Administration for Children and Families, the part of the Department of Health and Human Services that includes the Office of Refugee Resettlement. [source]

Advance parole

A different USCIS travel document from the refugee travel document. The Form I-131 instructions say a refugee who is not yet a permanent resident must have a refugee travel document to re-enter unless they have an Advance Parole Document. [source]

APHIS

The Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service, part of the U.S. Department of Agriculture. For pet dogs it adds rules when a country is affected by foot-and-mouth disease or screwworm. CDC sets the rabies rules. [source]

ASC

An Application Support Center. USCIS schedules biometrics, such as fingerprints and a photograph, at an ASC after you file for a refugee travel document. [source, source]

CBP

U.S. Customs and Border Protection. ORR says CBP admits refugees at the port of entry. A DHS officer at the port decides whether a person with a refugee travel document may come back in. [source, source]

CDC

The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. It sets the U.S. rules for bringing dogs and cats into the country, including rabies rules for dogs. [source, source]

CDC Dog Import Form

The free online form CDC requires for each dog entering the United States. You show the receipt to the airline and to Customs and Border Protection. [source, source]

CFR

The Code of Federal Regulations, where federal rules are published. The Social Security rule cited on this site is 20 CFR 422.103. [source]

Church World Service (CWS)

The organisation that operates RSC Africa. The Embassy names Church World Service as one of the two USRAP referral partners in South Africa. [source]

DALRRD

Older South African pages say DALRRD or DAFF. Export certification of animals is now on the Department of Agriculture website. A state veterinarian, not a private vet, signs the official export certificate. [source, source]

Department of Justice (DOJ)

The U.S. Department of Justice. USCIS says immigration legal help should come from an attorney or from an accredited representative of a DOJ-recognised organisation. [source]

DHA

The South African Department of Home Affairs. Its website is the official place for civic services such as passports and identity documents. [source]

DHS

The U.S. Department of Homeland Security. USCIS and CBP are part of it. When a Social Security number is requested in the immigration process, DHS sends the data to SSA. [source, source]

EAD

An Employment Authorization Document, the card that shows you may work. USCIS produces one for principal refugees and family who arrive with them. [source]

Executive Order (EO)

An order signed by the President. The refugee admission determinations on this site refer to Executive Order 14204. [source]

Federal Register (FR)

The official daily journal of the U.S. government. A citation such as 91 FR 31645 means volume 91, page 31645. [source]

Fiscal year (FY)

For these refugee ceilings, the federal fiscal year runs from 1 October to 30 September. FY2026 is 1 October 2025 to 30 September 2026. [source]

Fish and Wildlife Service (FWS)

The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service. USDA tells people importing a pet cat to check CDC, this service, and the state they are going to. [source]

Foot-and-mouth disease (FMD)

A livestock disease. APHIS has extra dog-import rules if the country of origin is affected. The disease-status page checked on 24 October 2026 does not name South Africa. [source, source]

Form N-470

Application to Preserve Residence for Naturalization Purposes. For some permanent residents it can keep continuous residence for naturalisation during qualifying work abroad. It is a different form from the refugee travel document. [source]

Green card (LPR)

A green card is proof of lawful permanent resident status. Refugees must apply on Form I-485 after one year of physical presence. [source]

I-131

USCIS Form I-131, the application for a refugee travel document and for some other travel documents. [source]

I-485

USCIS Form I-485, the application refugees use to become permanent residents. [source]

I-571

The document number of the refugee travel document itself. USCIS says it can be used in place of a passport and is valid for 1 year, or until refugee or asylee status expires, whichever comes first. [source]

I-9

Form I-9, the form an employer uses to check identity and permission to work. A refugee I-94 is acceptable for 90 days after arrival. [source]

I-94

The arrival and departure record. For a refugee it shows admission under section 207 and is evidence of status and of permission to work. [source, source]

IATA

The International Air Transport Association. Airlines use its Container Requirement 1 to judge the size of a dog or cat crate. [source]

INA

The Immigration and Nationality Act. Section 207 is the section on refugee admissions. [source]

IOM

The International Organization for Migration. The State Department manual uses this name. The October 2026 Embassy page for this programme does not name IOM as the office that books refugee travel. It names RSC Africa, operated by Church World Service. [source, source]

Matching Grant

An ORR alternative to TANF or Refugee Cash Assistance. Places are limited and the service period is 240 days. [source]

Medicaid

A joint federal and state health programme. ORR says refugees may be eligible. Refugee Medical Assistance is for people who are not eligible for Medicaid. [source]

Microchip

For a dog that has been in a high-risk rabies country, CDC requires a chip a universal scanner can read, implanted before the rabies vaccine. The number must appear on the forms. [source]

OCVI

An official certificate of veterinary inspection. Florida uses this name for an animal health certificate. The state page gives a 30-day window, with exceptions that include a privately owned pet travelling with its owner. [source]

ORR

The Office of Refugee Resettlement, inside ACF. It runs initial resettlement and refugee cash and medical assistance. [source]

PIR

The Program of Initial Resettlement. ORR's name for support in the first period after arrival, up to 90 days, which it took over on 1 January 2026. [source]

POMS

SSA's Program Operations Manual System, the staff instructions that say which documents a refugee can use for a Social Security card. [source]

Presidential Determination (PD)

A Presidential Determination sets the refugee admissions ceiling for a fiscal year. PD 2026-14 and PD 2026-24 are the ones cited on this site. [source, source]

PRM

The Bureau of Population, Refugees, and Migration at the Department of State. The Embassy FAQ uses this name. The February 2025 Embassy notice used the mailbox PretoriaPRMInfo@state.gov. [source, source, source]

Rabies serology titer

A blood test that shows whether a dog responded to the rabies vaccine. For a foreign-vaccinated dog from a high-risk country, a valid result from a CDC-approved laboratory is what lets the dog avoid a 28-day quarantine. [source, source]

RCA

Refugee Cash Assistance. Cash help from ORR if you are not eligible for TANF or SSI. For an eligibility date on or after 1 January 2026, ORR's current letter says up to 12 months. [source, source]

Re-entry permit

A travel document for lawful permanent residents, requested on Form I-131. The instructions say lengthy or frequent absences without one can support a finding that permanent residence was abandoned. [source]

Reception and Placement (R and P)

The older name for the initial support that ORR now calls the Program of Initial Resettlement. [source]

Refugee travel document

Also called Form I-571. The document USCIS issues so a refugee can travel abroad and ask to return to the United States. You apply on Form I-131. [source, source]

Resettlement agency

The local organisation assigned to meet you and help with the first weeks after arrival. ORR also calls this a local resettlement provider. [source]

RMA

Refugee Medical Assistance. Health cover from ORR for people who are not eligible for Medicaid. From 1 October 2026 it can last up to 12 months for an eligibility date on or after 1 January 2026. [source, source]

RSC Africa

Resettlement Support Center Africa, operated by Church World Service. The Embassy names it as a USRAP partner for case processing and for booking refugee travel to the United States. [source, source]

Screwworm

A parasite of warm-blooded animals. If APHIS lists the country as affected, a dog needs a government vet certificate of inspection within 5 days before shipment. The disease-status page checked on 24 October 2026 does not name South Africa. [source, source]

SNAP

The Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program, the federal food assistance programme. ORR says refugees may be eligible. [source, source]

SSA

The Social Security Administration, the agency that issues Social Security numbers and cards. [source]

SSI

Supplemental Security Income, a federal cash benefit for some older or disabled people. ORR lists it among benefits refugees may qualify for. [source]

SSN

A Social Security number. Refugees can be assigned one from the immigration record, or they can apply at an SSA office. [source, source]

State veterinarian

In South Africa, the official government veterinarian who can endorse an export health certificate. A private vet can prepare the animal but cannot give the official certificate. [source, source, source]

TANF

Temporary Assistance for Needy Families, the state cash programme for some families with children. ORR says refugees may be eligible. [source]

UIQ

The USRAP Intake Questionnaire. The online form the U.S. Embassy in South Africa tells people to complete if they want to be considered for a referral. [source]

USCIS

U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services, the DHS agency that interviews refugee applicants and decides applications such as Form I-131 and Form I-485. [source, source]

USDA

The U.S. Department of Agriculture. APHIS is the part of USDA that sets some pet-import rules. [source]

USRAP

The U.S. Refugee Admissions Program, the programme through which refugees are referred, interviewed and resettled. [source]

Visa 93

A code that can appear on an I-94 for a refugee who followed to join family. ORR lists it as one way to prove refugee status for benefits. [source]
