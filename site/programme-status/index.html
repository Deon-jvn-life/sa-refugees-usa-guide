Programme status and caps

FY2026 (1 October 2025 to 30 September 2026)

  • PD 2026-14 of 21 May 2026 raised the FY2026 ceiling from 7,500 (set by PD 2025-13) to 17,500 [source].
  • The additional admissions are allocated among Afrikaners from South Africa, consistent with Executive Order 14204 [source].
  • Published 27 May 2026, 91 FR 31645 [source].

FY2027 determination

  • Yes, there is one. PD 2026-24 of 25 September 2026 authorises up to 17,500 refugee admissions in FY2027 [source].
  • Admissions are "primarily" allocated among Afrikaners from South Africa under Executive Order 14204 [source].
  • Published 2 October 2026 [source].

Who runs what

  • The State Department identifies and refers applicants; USCIS interviews and checks them; CDC-approved doctors do the medical exam; CBP admits refugees at the port of entry; ORR starts services on arrival [source].
  • ORR took over initial resettlement (now the Program of Initial Resettlement) on 1 January 2026 [source].

How many places are left in FY2027: not stated by source.
