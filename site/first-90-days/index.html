First 90 days

Who helps you

ORR coordinates placement and initial resettlement services up to 90 days through the Program of Initial Resettlement (PIR). Refugees are typically assigned a local resettlement provider [source].

PIR is delivered by eight organisations: Catholic Charities USA, Church World Service, Ethiopian Community Development Council, Global Refuge, HIAS, International Rescue Committee, U.S. Committee for Refugees and Immigrants, and World Relief [source].

What the provider helps with

  • Airport welcome
  • Temporary or longer-term housing with food, necessities, furnishings and household items (hotels may be used where housing is limited)
  • Personal case management and a resettlement plan
  • Connections to cash and medical assistance
  • Referrals for the domestic medical screening, jobs, social services and school enrolment
[source]

Do these early

  • Sign up for benefits immediately: they are time-limited. See Benefits checklist [source].
  • Get the ORR-funded domestic medical screening soon after arrival [source].
  • Check your Social Security card arrives.
  • Keep your I-94 safe; it proves refugee status and work authorisation [source, source].

Minimum service period of 30 days: not stated by the ACF page; it says services "up to 90 days" [source].
