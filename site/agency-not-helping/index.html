When your agency is not helping

Step 1: use the provider's grievance process

ORR requires programmes to have grievance policies so you can raise concerns, and says it reviews complaints with the implementing organisations [source].

Step 2: contact your State Refugee Coordinator

ACF publishes a State Refugee Coordinator and State Refugee Health Coordinator for each state, with email and phone [source]. Find yours on ACF Key State Contacts. They can also give a list of providers in your area [source].

Step 3: ORR

ORR's Program of Initial Resettlement contact: DRSAPrograms@acf.hhs.gov [source].

Gaps

Response times for complaints: not stated by source. Contact names change; check the ACF page (dated 18 September 2026) [source].
