South African refugees coming to the USA: where are you in the journey?

Start the official online intake questionnaireRead our guide to the questionnaire first

A plain-language, factual guide for South Africans being processed through, or recently resettled by, the U.S. Refugee Admissions Program (USRAP). Open Start here for the pages in time order. Words such as USRAP are explained in the glossary.
