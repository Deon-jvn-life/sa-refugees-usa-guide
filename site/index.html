South African refugees coming to the USA: where are you in the journey?

A plain-language, factual guide for South Africans being processed through, or recently resettled by, the U.S. Refugee Admissions Program (USRAP). Pick your stage below.

  • The FY2026 refugee ceiling was raised from 7,500 to 17,500 by Presidential Determination 2026-14 [source].
  • The FY2027 ceiling is up to 17,500 under Presidential Determination 2026-24 [source].
  • Since 1 January 2026 the Office of Refugee Resettlement (ORR) runs initial resettlement [source].
This is general information, not legal advice. Refugee status questions can affect your status: confirm with your resettlement agency, an accredited representative or an immigration attorney.
